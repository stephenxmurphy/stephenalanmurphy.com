Centre for Aerospace Research · Mechanical Engineering Intern

Boeing Sensorcraft

Given the outer shape of the aircraft, I designed all internal and external components of this joined-wing aircraft including the composite layup, internal structure, integrated electronics, and custom-machined 6061 interfaces to secure everything together. This 3 m wingspan, small-scale aircraft was used to develop a quantitative model of the buckling behavior of the aft wings in flight.

Centre for Aerospace Research · Mechanical Engineering Intern

Pneumatic Launcher for <20 kg UAVs

I designed a fixed-wing drone launcher for aircraft up to 20 kg and takeoff speeds up to 20 m/s. After placing the drone on the launch platform and connecting it to the launch hook, the launcher pneumatically fires a piston from the main barrel, propelling the aircraft to takeoff speed. I designed the system to be modular so it could be disassembled, stored in a case, and transported by vehicle.

I also created a multilevel Simulink model to simulate the behavior of the pneumatic launcher.

Institute of Ocean Sciences · Oceanographic Modelling Intern

Oceanographic Heat Flux Modeling in MATLAB

At the Institute of Ocean Sciences, I created a MATLAB model of heat flux through the Broughton Archipelago using data gathered from remote weather stations. During this project, I gained significant experience in MATLAB programming and modeling scientific data. The model results were used to predict annual salmon throughput for environmental sustainability research.

I also joined a week-long scientific cruise to collect water samples throughout the Strait of Georgia on the west coast of Canada.

University of Victoria · Graduate Student

Master of Engineering: Zinc-air Battery Modeling

For my master's project, I developed a thermo-electrochemical model of a novel zinc-air battery configuration to evaluate temperature and mass-flow effects on cell potential and total power efficiency. Zinc-air batteries are one candidate for large-scale energy storage to address the misaligned timing between production and demand of renewable energy.

My model was written in Python using Jupyter Notebooks and was used to understand trade-offs between geometrical and thermal parameters of the system.

University of Victoria AERO Team · President & Aeronautical Lead

Aircraft Design and Composite Construction

At UVic AERO, I designed fixed-wing aircraft to compete in an aerial surveying challenge at the Unmanned Systems Canada Student UAS Competition. I used XFLR5 to determine wing loading, trim condition, and lift distribution, then designed the aircraft in SOLIDWORKS, molded the aircraft from composite materials using vacuum infusion, assembled it, and performed flight testing.

My team placed 3rd nationally while I was Aeronautical Lead and 2nd nationally while I was Team President.
