Shield AI · Sr. Staff Mechanical Engineer

X-BAT Wing-fold

In my role as Sr. Staff Mechanical Engineer at Shield AI, I work on actuated systems and various mechanical integrations as part of the Mechanical Systems team. My biggest project to date has been the conceptual design and analysis of the X-BAT wing fold system, one of the most ambitious fighter jet wing fold systems in the last several decades.

This wing fold system uses hydraulic actuators to fold a very large portion of the wingspan into an extremely small width and height to enable transport of the X-BAT vehicle on common U.S. Air Force transport aircraft. It has a double-hinge design coupled to a large bell crank and dual hydraulic actuators to rotate each side of the wing through roughly 180 degrees of motion. I owned the wing-fold system including mechanism design, planform impacts, door layouts and down-locks, structural lock-pins, actuator sizing, and the hydraulic subsystem.

SkySafe · Mechanical Design Manager

Gen 2 Counter-UAS Hardware

As Mechanical Design Manager at SkySafe, I led the mechanical design and program management of their next generation counter-UAS systems. These systems comprise compute hardware, a software-defined radio, and antennas that listen to and reverse-engineer drone communications to gain information such as GPS coordinates of the drone and pilot. SkySafe systems are used where drone traffic is undesirable, including borders, stadiums, airports, and prisons.

MAVinci · Mechanical Engineer

3-axis CNC Mill Design and Construction

When I joined MAVinci, I needed a way to prototype large foam parts quickly while designing their next generation drone. Commercial CNC mills were expensive, had small working areas, and were designed primarily for metals. I therefore designed and built a large 3-axis CNC router that reduced prototyping lead times from roughly two weeks to two days with significant cost savings.

I machined my aircraft designs on this CNC, assembled them, flight tested each prototype, and iterated on each design.
