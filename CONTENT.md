# Shield AI

## X-BAT Wing-fold

**Role line**  
Shield AI · Sr. Staff Mechanical Engineer

**Project title**  
X-BAT Wing-fold

**Description — paragraph 1**  
In my role as Sr. Staff Mechanical Engineer at Shield AI, I work on actuated systems and various mechanical integrations as part of the Mechanical Systems team. My biggest project to date has been the conceptual design and analysis of the X-BAT wing fold system, one of the most ambitious fighter jet wing fold systems in the last several decades.

**Description — paragraph 2**  
This wing fold system uses hydraulic actuators to fold a very large portion of the wingspan into an extremely small width and height to enable transport of the X-BAT vehicle on common U.S. Air Force transport aircraft. It has a double-hinge design coupled to a large bell crank and dual hydraulic actuators to rotate each side of the wing through roughly 180 degrees of motion. I owned the wing-fold system including mechanism design, planform impacts, door layouts and down-locks, structural lock-pins, actuator sizing, and the hydraulic subsystem.

### Media captions

1. X-BAT: Earth Is Our Runway.
2. X-BAT.
3. X-BAT vertical flight concept.
4. The X-BAT wing-fold concept builds on prior folding-wing aircraft while pushing folded package size significantly further.

**Description — paragraph 1**  
I designed a fixed-wing drone launcher for aircraft up to 20 kg and takeoff speeds up to 20 m/s. # University of Victoria

## Master of Engineering: Zinc-air Battery Modeling

**Role line**  
University of Victoria · Graduate Student

**Project title**  
Master of Engineering: Zinc-air Battery Modeling

**Description — paragraph 1**  
For my master's project, I developed a thermo-electrochemical model of a novel zinc-air battery configuration to evaluate temperature and mass-flow effects on cell potential and total power efficiency. Zinc-air batteries are one candidate for large-scale energy storage to address the misaligned timing between production and demand of renewable energy.

**Description — paragraph 2**  
My model was written in Python using Jupyter Notebooks and was used to understand trade-offs between geometrical and thermal parameters of the system.

### Media captions

3. High-level architecture of the modeled system with independent discharge and charge stacks.
4. Example analysis of reversible cell potentials for zinc-air batteries.
